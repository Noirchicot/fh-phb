*(Rituels Noirs — la magie impériale)*

**Dark Rituals** are large, collaborative magical workings that reach past the spell list — multi-caster ceremonies that bend the world's deep structure at a terrible price, paid in pooled **[Destiny](fates-hand-mechanic.md)**, in life, and often in blood. They are the province of **Imperial magic**, rooted in the **Moisson** of Karagall — the trade of souls and lives for power. Each is gated by **skill checks**, and they bite back when they fail.

## 1. How a ritual resolves (the template)

Throughout the ritual, every participating caster spends a **magic action each round**. **Leadership or expertise is a prerequisite for the Help action** — individuals who are **at least Novice** in it, and who are neither main nor support casters, may still Help, but they do **not** count toward the required level total.

Each ritual is defined by the following:

- **Level** — the combined total level of the main and support casters.
- **Main caster(s)** — minimum 1, of the required spellcasting subclass(es). They make a check (usually **Arcana** or **Religion**) against a DC; the skill used and the difficulty vary by ritual.
- **Support caster(s)** — minimum 1, of the required spellcasting class(es). They make a check (a skill or tool check) against a DC; the skill used and the difficulty vary by ritual.
- **Destiny cost** — the Destiny points spent to complete the ritual; the main casters decide how the cost is split between participants.
- **Life cost** — necrotic damage the casters pay to fuel the ritual; the dice and scaling **vary by ritual** (by sacrifices, seals, material, area, or duration). The main casters decide how it is distributed.
- **Failure** — a botched rite turns on its casters. The GM sets a consequence to fit it; where an entry below reads *varies*, that is the rule — improvise something fittingly dire.

## 2. The Rituals

### Create Major Sealed Prison
*Level 30 ritual*

- **Main caster(s):** Moonkeeper, Land Druid — **Arcana DC 22**
- **Support caster(s):** Bard, Wizard, Sorcerer, Druid, Cleric — **Survival DC 22**
- **Prerequisites:** the sacrifice of 4 willing sentient beings + 10,000 gp
- **Destiny cost:** 10
- **Life cost:** **2X·d12** necrotic, where **X = the number of sacrifices** (so 8d12 at 4 sacrifices)
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** Transfers a volume of the material plane into a **prison demiplane** — a cube 100 feet on a side for each sacrifice. The demiplane can be reached from the material plane only through a **gate**: a disk of **force** that appears where the ritual ends, its radius scaling with the prison (the more sacrifices, the larger the gate).

The gate is held shut by **seals** — carved **silver disks one foot across, each set with an encrusted ruby** — that adhere to its face. There are as many seals as there were sacrifices, and each bears a **password** which, spoken, locks or unlocks the gate.

The sacrifices rise as **ghosts** that guard access to the seals on the material plane, within a 50-foot radius.

**Endurance & degradation.** So long as its seals take no structural damage, a Major Sealed Prison is **all but eternal**. Break one or more seals and it begins to **degrade** — and the more seals are broken, the faster it decays. If every seal (or every guardian ghost) is destroyed, the demiplane collapses and its contents are pushed back out onto the material plane.

### Create Sealed Prison
*Level 10 ritual*

- **Main caster(s):** Moonkeeper *or* Land Druid — **Arcana DC 18**
- **Support caster(s):** Wizard, Sorcerer, Cleric, Druid — **Survival DC 18**
- **Prerequisites:** the sacrifice of **X non-sentient creatures**
- **Destiny cost:** **1 per sacrifice**
- **Life cost:** **Xd6** necrotic, where **X = the number of sacrifices**
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** As *Create Major Sealed Prison*, but smaller in every dimension — a cube **30 feet on a side** per sacrifice, a smaller force-gate, and silver-and-ruby seals to match. Because the sacrifices are **not sentient, they leave no ghosts**; the seals are instead watched by **gargoyle guardians** — one per seal, within a 50-foot radius.

**Endurance & degradation.** A Sealed Prison is **not** eternal: even with its seals intact, it **decays over a span of weeks** and must be renewed (see *Repair Seal*) or it collapses on its own. Broken seals hasten the end, as above.

### Repair Seal
*Level 10 ritual — repairs the seals of a Sealed Prison, **Major** or not*

- **Main caster(s):** of the prison's own tradition — check vs the **prison's Main DC** (Arcana 22 for a Major prison, 18 for a Minor)
- **Support caster(s):** as the prison required
- **Destiny cost:** **⌈ the prison's creation Destiny ÷ the number of seals repaired ⌉** — for a Major prison, ⌈10 ÷ seals repaired⌉ (10 to repair a single seal, 3 to repair all four at once)
- **Life cost:** **Xd12** necrotic, where **X = the number of seals repaired**
- **Prerequisites:** **at least one seal must still be in place**; **1,000 gp per seal**; **no sacrifice if the seal's guardian still remains** (the **ghost** of a Major prison, the **gargoyle** of a Minor), otherwise **one sacrifice of the prison's own kind** — a sentient being for a ghost, a non-sentient creature for a gargoyle — to raise a replacement guardian
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** Reforges broken or damaged **seals** — fresh silver-and-ruby disks, each keyed to a new password and bound to the gate — halting the prison's degradation and restoring it toward full endurance. A prison can never bear more seals than it had sacrifices.

### Conjure Minor Crimson Shroud
*Level 5 ritual*

- **Main caster(s):** Necromancer Wizard, or a Cleric of Karagall — **Religion DC 15**
- **Support caster(s):** **Hunting DC 15**
- **Materials:** **red sand**, 25 gp per cup
- **Destiny cost:** **1 per cup**
- **Life cost:** **1d4 per cup**
- **Area:** a **5-ft radius per cup** of red sand
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** Conjures a single small pocket of Crimson Shroud, sized by the red sand spent. It **drifts of its own accord** toward the nearest living things (20 ft per round) and **cannot be steered**. The pocket disperses at dawn, or when the ritual's hold breaks. For what the mist does, see **[Effect of the Crimson Shroud](primordial-forces.md#part-2-the-crimson-shroud)**.

### Conjure Major Crimson Shroud
*Level 10 ritual*

- **Main caster(s):** Necromancer Wizard, or a Cleric of Karagall — **Religion DC 18**
- **Support caster(s):** **Hunting DC 18**
- **Materials:** **red sand by the sack** (1 sack = 10 cups = 250 gp)
- **Destiny cost:** **3 per sack**
- **Life cost:** **1d8 per sack**, then **1d6 per 20 ft moved**
- **Area:** a **20-ft radius per sack**, to a **maximum of 100 ft** (5 sacks)
- **Movement:** **controlled** — the casters may move the bank up to **20 ft per round**
- **Sacrifices:** none (beyond whatever the mist itself kills)
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** As *Conjure Minor Crimson Shroud*, but raised as a **larger bank** and placed under the casters' **control**, steered across the field rather than left to wander. See **[Effect of the Crimson Shroud](primordial-forces.md#part-2-the-crimson-shroud)**.

### Create Harvest Chalice
*Level 10 ritual*

- **Main caster(s):** Necromancer Wizard, or a Cleric of Karagall — **Religion DC 16**
- **Support caster(s):** any arcane or divine caster — **DC 16**
- **Destiny cost:** 2
- **Life cost:** 2d12 necrotic
- **Prerequisites:** **1 sentient sacrifice**; a silver-and-gold vessel worth **1,000 gp**
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** Forges a **[Harvest Chalice](magic-items.md#harvest-chalice)** *(Calice de Moisson)* with a capacity of **10 souls**. What the chalice *does* is on the [item](magic-items.md#harvest-chalice).

### Create Major Harvest Chalice
*Level 30 ritual*

- **Main caster(s):** High Priest of Karagall — **Religion DC 22**
- **Support caster(s):** Necromancer Wizard, Sorcerer, Cleric — **DC 22**
- **Destiny cost:** 10
- **Life cost:** **2X·d12** necrotic, where **X = the number of sacrifices**
- **Prerequisites:** **X sentient sacrifices** (each grants **25 souls** of capacity, to a hard cap of **10,000**); a silver-and-gold vessel sized to match — 1 ft of diameter per 100 souls (see the [item](magic-items.md#harvest-chalice)'s value table)
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** Forges a **Major [Harvest Chalice](magic-items.md#harvest-chalice)** — capacity **25 × (sacrifices)** souls, up to **10,000** (a 100-foot silver-and-gold sphere, the stuff of the old Ziggurat reservoirs).

### Forest Walking, Minor
*Level 5 ritual*

- **Main / Support:** **Religion / Survival DC 14**
- **Duration:** **4 to 8 hours** (in 4-hour increments)
- **Destiny cost:** **1 per 4-hour increment**
- **Life cost:** **1d4 per 4-hour increment**
- **Prerequisites:** 2 corpses to animate
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** Where druids once walked the living wood by its consent, Imperial magic takes the road by terror. This rite raises **2 zombies** that march ahead for the duration; within a **10-foot radius** of them the **forest cowers away**, opening a passage the party can follow. When the duration ends — or the zombies are destroyed — the wood closes back in.

### Forest Walking, Major
*Level 15 ritual*

- **Main / Support:** **Religion / Survival DC 18**
- **Duration:** **4 to 8 hours** (in 4-hour increments)
- **Destiny cost:** **3 per 4-hour increment**
- **Life cost:** **1d12 per 4-hour increment**
- **Prerequisites:** 4 corpses to animate
- **Failure:** varies

**Effect.** As *Forest Walking, Minor*, but raises **4 zombies** and clears a **50-foot radius** — a road wide enough for a column, not just a single file.

### Soulforge an Item
*Level scales with the item's tier — Common 0 · Uncommon 5 · Rare 10 · Very Rare 16 · Legendary 30*

The rite that **binds power into a crafted body** — the ritual face of **[Soulforge Crafting](soulforge-crafting.md)**. A lone **Soulforger** can attempt the lower tiers; run as a Dark Ritual, a **circle pools its levels** to reach the higher ones. The full system (Structure crafted, Essence infused, Catalyst slotted in as-is — plus costs, times, mishaps and boons) lives on the [Soulforge Crafting](soulforge-crafting.md) page; this is its ritual frame.

- **Main caster:** the **Soulforger** (Necromancer Wizard, Cleric of Karagall, or any trained in the Forge) makes the **single check** — **Soulforging Tools (CHA) vs the item's Soulforge DC** (**DC ≈ 12 + 2·PP** of the highest catalyst — 13 Common to 34 Epic+).
- **Secondary:** the **attunee** — the one the finished item **attunes to**. Present for the whole casting, lends their **level** to the pooled total, and **may be the Soulforger** (nothing stops the forger from keeping the item for themselves). If they are not the one rolling and are **at least Novice with Soulforging Tools**, they may take the **Help** action (advantage on the forger's check). *(The base body must already be crafted — Artisan's Tools, DMG 2024 price — see [Soulforge Crafting](soulforge-crafting.md) phase 3.)*
- **The circle:** every other participant lending their level must be **at least Novice with Soulforging Tools** — the **attunee alone may be exempt**, since they are there to receive the bind, not to work it. The **Soulsmith** (the forge's owner) may lead the rite, assist it, or stay **outside it entirely** if the parties agree.
- **Destiny cost:** none required — the price is the **gold forge cost + the Soulgem(s)** ([Soulforge Crafting](soulforge-crafting.md)); casters *may* spend Destiny to aid the check.
- **Life cost:** the forge feeds on its makers — **Nd6 necrotic**, where **N = the PP of the item's highest Soulgem** (1 Common → 11 Epic+). Rolled as **one pool**, which the **Soulforger distributes as they see fit** among the assistants and the attunee.
- **Casting time:** the item's **creation time** (a few hours → 1 month, by tier).
- **Failure:** roll on the **[Soulforge mishaps](soulforge-crafting.md#soulforge-mishaps-boons)** table.

**Effect.** Completes — or **grows, never replaces** — a **soulforged item**: the crafted **body** (Structure) receives the **Soulgems** (its Power Supply) and the **catalysts** slotted in raw, each granting a property within the [three laws of equilibrium](soulforge-crafting.md#the-three-laws-of-equilibrium). A check that **beats the DC by 5+** (or a natural 20) also rolls a **boon**. Full rules: [Soulforge Crafting](soulforge-crafting.md).
