# Species

The species of Fate's Hand 5+ — traits, Base Destiny and skill-point bumps. The list follows the **SRD 5.2** roster, plus two species native to Nymedes: the **Araag** and the **Eluzi**.

> [!note] No ability bonuses from species
> Species no longer grant characteristic modifiers. **Ability score increases come from your background** (choose three abilities: +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1). After all creation modifiers, the maximum in a characteristic is **18**; at higher levels the cap becomes **20**. Backgrounds never raise the cap.
>
> *Looking for the older species (Aasimar, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Eladrin, Firbolg) and the old racial modifiers? They live on in the archive for existing tables: Species — archived version.*

> [!info] Skill-oriented species
> Only the **Araag** and the **Human** gain [skill points](skills-and-tools.md): **+2 at levels 3 and 6** (the Araag also gains **+2 at creation**). No other species grants skill points.

## [Dragonborn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751435-dragonborn)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Draconic Ancestry (choose a dragon type), Breath Weapon, Damage Resistance (ancestry type), Darkvision 60 ft, Draconic Flight *(level 5)*

## [Dwarf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751436-dwarf)
- **Base Destiny:** 1 — *Focus Is All: advantage on a Destiny roll once per long rest (take highest).*
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Darkvision 120 ft, Dwarven Resilience (poison resistance, advantage vs Poisoned), Dwarven Toughness (+1 HP per level), Stonecunning (Tremorsense 60 ft on stone)

## [Elf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751437-elf)
- **Base Destiny:** 1 — *Precision Is Paramount: once per long rest, an elf may take the average (rounded down) on a Destiny roll instead of rolling it.*
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Elven Lineage (Drow / High Elf / Wood Elf), Fey Ancestry, Trance, Darkvision 60 ft; ~~Keen Senses~~ *(removed — FH)*
- **Special:** must spend at least one skill point in **Arcana** and **Survival**.

## [Gnome](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751438-gnome)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Gnomish Cunning (advantage on INT/WIS/CHA saves), Gnomish Lineage (Forest / Rock), Darkvision 60 ft

## [Goliath](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751439-goliath)

## [Halfling](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751440-halfling)

## Araag *(Fate's Hand)*
The **Araags** are the fiend-marked children of Nymedes — born of Karagall's dying followers as his Moisson collapsed the human population. Once counted as tieflings, they are now a **species of their own**, shaped by soul-craft and the long shadow of the Empire. Lore: L'Empire Araag · L'Éveil & Ascension des Eluzi.

- **Base Destiny:** 2 *(working value — full rebuild in progress by Eric)*
- **Abilities** *(canon de travail)*:
  - **Soulforged Affinity** — a power tied to [Soulforging](soulforge-crafting.md) *(to be defined)*
  - **Necrotic Resistance**
  - **Darkvision 60 ft**
  - **+2 Skill Points at creation, +2 at levels 3 and 6**

## Eluzi *(Aberration — an ascended state)*
The **Eluzi** are not born — they are **made**. A being who drinks **1,000 souls** undergoes the **Crimson Awakening** (see the [Calice de Moisson](magic-items.md#harvest-chalice)): it awakens to **The Devil — Arcana XV** and its body **mutates into an Aberration** — white-skinned and masked, a **race apart**. It keeps the memory of what it was and becomes something else; from here the road of cumulative torpor leads, for a rare handful, to **Somatic Ascension** (pure energy in a corporeal shell).

- **Creature type becomes Aberration.** It retains its original species' traits unless the table rules otherwise.
- **Reached through play**, not chosen at creation — any species, including Araags, can in the Third Age potentially awaken to AvA and become Eluzi.
- **Mechanical traits (Base Destiny, abilities) — stat block to be defined.**
- Lore: L'Éveil & Ascension des Eluzi · Les Forces Primordiales de Nymedes.
