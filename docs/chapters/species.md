# Species

The species of Fate's Hand 5+ — traits, Base Destiny and skill-point bumps. The list follows the **SRD 5.2** roster, plus species native to Nymedes: the **Araag**, and — specific to **Theymoron** — the **Loroka** and the **Elestu**.

> [!note] No ability bonuses from species
> Species no longer grant characteristic modifiers. **Ability score increases come from your background** (choose three abilities: +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1). After all creation modifiers, the maximum in a characteristic is **18**; at higher levels the cap becomes **20**. Backgrounds never raise the cap.
>
> *Looking for the older species (Aasimar, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Eladrin, Firbolg) and the old racial modifiers? They live on in the archive for existing tables: Species — archived version.*

> [!note] Base Destiny, languages & speed — flat across every species
> **Base Destiny is 2 for every species.** Every species gets **2 languages** at creation (Common + one of your choice — standard SRD, unchanged) and keeps its **SRD-standard speed** (Gnome/Dwarf/Halfling stay at 30 ft). Not every species has a dedicated **Destiny-chosen power** — for now only **Elf**, **Human** and **Halfling** have one; the rest have none until a future power is written for them.

> [!info] Skill-oriented species
> A one-time skill proficiency at creation (**Skillful**) goes to **Human** and **Araag**. A recurring [skill-point](skills-and-tools.md) bump (**Fast Learner** — +2 at levels 3 and 6) goes to **Araag** and **Elestu** only. No other species grants either.

## [Dragonborn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751435-dragonborn)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Draconic Ancestry (choose a dragon type), Breath Weapon, Damage Resistance (ancestry type), Darkvision 60 ft, Draconic Flight *(level 5)*

## [Dwarf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751436-dwarf)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Darkvision 120 ft, Dwarven Resilience (poison resistance, advantage vs Poisoned), Dwarven Toughness (+1 HP per level), Stonecunning (Tremorsense 60 ft on stone)

## [Elf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751437-elf)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*:
  - **Elven Lineage** — Drow, High Elf or Wood Elf
  - **Fey Ancestry** — advantage against being charmed; magic can't put you to sleep
  - **Trance** — 4 hours of light meditation replace a long rest's sleep
  - **Keen Senses** — proficiency in Perception
  - **Darkvision** — 60 ft
- **Destiny chosen — elf:** +2 to your Destiny pool.

Elves are truly immortal — and vanishingly rare, for elves cannot conceive with one another. Only a union with a human or an Araag quickens an elven womb, and what is born is Elestu, not elf. The oldest among them eventually withdraw from the world: some slip into centuries-long sleep, others into a quiet madness no healer's art can touch. A dying elf's soulgem is said to be the finest a soulforger will ever hold — scholars whisper it still carries a splinter of a sleeping god's memory, sealed away since the First Age.

## [Gnome](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751438-gnome)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Gnomish Cunning (advantage on INT/WIS/CHA saves), Gnomish Lineage (Forest / Rock), Darkvision 60 ft

## [Goliath](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751439-goliath)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Giant Ancestry (choose a boon), Large Form *(level 5)*, Powerful Build; Speed 35 ft

## [Halfling](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751440-halfling)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Brave, Halfling Nimbleness, Luck, Naturally Stealthy
- **Destiny chosen — halfling:** advantage on [Chaos rolls](chaos-tables.md).

Small, unhurried, and hard to rattle — halfling communities tend to outlast the empires around them, quietly farming the same valley for a thousand years while dynasties rise and fall on the other side of the ridge. Luck, they'll tell you, is just what patience looks like from the outside.

## Human
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*:
  - **Skillful** — proficiency in one skill of your choice
  - **Versatile** — gain an Origin feat of your choice
- **Destiny chosen — human:** +1 Destiny Point per day. *(unique to Human — no other lineage shares this)*

The founders' blood — ten thousand souls who walked out of the White Void a century ago and refused to vanish a second time. Human ambition built Theymoron's terraces and its armies both; wherever Nymedes still has room to grow, a human got there first and started arguing about who owns it.

## [Orc](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751442-orc)
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Adrenaline Rush, Relentless Endurance, Darkvision 120 ft

## Tiefling
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(SRD 5.2)*: Fiendish Legacy (Abyssal / Chthonic / Infernal), Otherworldly Presence (Thaumaturgy), Darkvision 60 ft

## Loroka *(Fate's Hand — Theymoron)*
The **Loroka** are the demi-orcs native to the lands that became Theymoron — the historical local population of the region, numerically comparable to the Araags before the founding, drawn heavily from the **Sira** (low imperial caste). Today they are the plurality of the Marche, both in the capital and in the liberated villages of the countryside. Lore: Theymoron.

- **Mechanically built as:** **Orc** on D&D Beyond (closest SRD 5.2 species), reflavored — see Theymoron demographics.
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(canon de travail)*:
  - **Relentless Endurance** — once per long rest, when reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, drop to 1 HP instead.
  - **Darkvision** — 120 ft
  - **Versatile** — gain an Origin feat of your choice
- No skill points *(consistent with the ruleset — only Araag & Elestu grant recurring skill points)*.

The old blood of the Theymoron highlands — farmers, drovers and terrace-workers who worked these valleys long before the Marche had a name. Once the plurality of a conquered people, they are now the plurality of a free city: soldiers when the Front calls for it, but merchants, roofers and rice-growers the rest of the year.

## Elestu *(Fate's Hand — Theymoron)*
The **Elestu** are the demi-elves of Theymoron — a small but present minority, drawn to the university, the Moonkeeper circles, and the university's practical-magic curriculum. Lore: Theymoron.

- **Mechanically built as:** **Elf** on D&D Beyond (closest SRD 5.2 species), reflavored.
- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(canon de travail)*:
  - **Fey Ancestry** — advantage on saves against being Charmed; magic can't put you to sleep.
  - **Trance** — 4 hours of light meditation replace a long rest's sleep.
  - **Keen Senses** — proficiency in Perception.
  - **Darkvision** — 60 ft
  - **Fast Learner** — +2 skill points at levels 3 and 6 *(recurring — see [skill points](skills-and-tools.md))*

Children of scattered pairings across Theymoron's university quarter and its Moonkeeper circles — too mortal for the elves, too strange for the humans, the Elestu built their own quiet place between both worlds. Most end up bards, scholars, or both at once.

## Araag *(Fate's Hand)*
The **Araags** are the fiend-marked children of Nymedes — born of Karagall's dying followers as his Moisson collapsed the human population. Once counted as tieflings, they are now a **species of their own**, shaped by soul-craft and the long shadow of the Empire. Lore: L'Empire Araag · L'Éveil & Ascension des Eluzi.

- **Base Destiny:** 2
- **Abilities** *(canon de travail)*:
  - **Skillful** — proficiency in one skill of your choice
  - **Fast Learner** — +2 skill points at levels 3 and 6
  - **Soulforged Affinity** — you may Body Forge a Soulgem into your own flesh at no cost in hit points *(cf. [Soulforge Crafting](soulforge-crafting.md) — "Araags pay nothing")*
  - **Necrotic Resistance**
  - **Darkvision** — 60 ft

Marked by the Harvest that unmade the last free humans, the Araags were once mistaken for tieflings — a resemblance that never sat easy with either people. They are common as dust within the Empire that shares their name, and stared at everywhere else: soul-touched, unmistakably other, and never quite trusted to explain why.
