# Circle Magic
*(Magie de Cercle — Circle Spells)*

Fate's Hand uses the **2024 Circle Magic / Circle Spells** system (the *Heroes of Faerûn* rules, credited in-world to the **Red Wizards of Thay**): a **primary caster**, backed by **secondary casters**, pours extra effort into a single spell to empower it with one **Circle Casting Option**. It is brutally strong — even a handful of low-level assistants can transform a spell.

> [!important] House rule (Fate's Hand)
> RAW, a secondary caster need only have the **Spellcasting or Pact Magic** feature. In Fate's Hand, **every cooperating caster must *also* possess the spell being cast** — a circle that unleashes a given spell is a circle of people who *all* know it.

## How a Circle Spell is cast
1. The **primary caster** takes the **Magic action** to begin a **qualifying spell** — casting time **1 action or 1 minute+** (no reaction / bonus-action spells) — and **maintains Concentration** until it resolves.
2. The primary chooses **one Circle Casting Option** (below).
3. One or more **secondary casters** assist. Each must have the **Spellcasting or Pact Magic** feature *(Fate's Hand house rule: **and must know the spell** being cast)*. **Any source of the feature qualifies** — casting granted by **species or a feat counts** *(Fate's Hand override of the RAW restriction)*.
4. On their turn, each secondary caster takes the **Magic action** to feed the casting — **spending a spell slot** if the option calls for it. This can stretch over **any length of time** while the primary holds Concentration.
5. When the **last secondary caster** acts, the spell **takes effect**, enhanced.

## Circle Casting Options *(choose one)*

| Option | What it does | Secondary-caster cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Augment** — *Range* | Adds **1,000 ft** of range, to a **maximum of 1 mile**. | Magic action |
| **Prolong** — *Duration* | **Extends the duration** (≈ **+1 hour** per contributing caster; up to ~24 h with a full circle). | a slot of **any level** |
| **Distribute** — *Concentration* | A **secondary caster holds Concentration** in the primary's place — freeing the primary to act, or to run several Concentration spells at once. | Magic action |
| **Expand** — *Area* | **Enlarges the area of effect — no maximum.** | a slot of **any level** |
| **Safeguard** — *Protect allies* | **Omits chosen 5-ft squares** from the area, sheltering allies inside your own AoE (Meteor Swarm, Wall of Fire, Hunger of Hadar…). | Magic action |
| **Supplant** — *Cost* | **Reduces the spell's gp material cost** (≈ 50 gp per contributing caster). | a slot of the **same or higher** level |

> [!note] RAW vs. the RPGBot pointer — read this before you argue numbers
> Two different things are cited on this page, and they don't carry equal weight:
> - **The official rules (RAW).** The *system itself* — primary + secondary casters, the Magic action, Concentration, and the **six Circle Casting Options** above — is **rules-as-written from *Heroes of Faerûn*** (2024). That book is the authority; its tables hold the **exact per-caster increments** for **Expand** (area) and **Prolong** (duration), which we summarise here only approximately (≈).
> - **The RPGBot guide (a pointer, not a rule).** The linked [RPGBot — 2024 Circle Spells Guide](https://rpgbot.net/2024-dnd/2024-dnd-circle-spells-guide/) is a **third-party reading** of those rules — handy for a plain-language overview, but it is **commentary, not canon**. Where it and *Heroes of Faerûn* ever disagree, **the book wins**.
>
> So: when a number matters at the table, **check *Heroes of Faerûn* directly**; treat the ≈ values and the RPGBot link as a convenience.
>
> **Metamagic.** Circle options **can combine with Metamagic**, but the official text is silent on order of operations (e.g. Prolong + Extend Spell) — settle that one at the table.
