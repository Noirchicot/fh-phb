# Arcana

## Drawing a Major Arcana at character creation

At character creation, draw **among the 22 Major Arcana** to determine its influence on the character's fate: [Random Tarot Card Generator](https://randomtarotcard.com/Death.html).

> [!note] One Arcana at a time
> **Only one Major Arcana is active at a time** — there is no stacking of powers. When you draw a new Major Arcana (on a Major Awakening), you **gain +1 to your Destiny Score and 10 temporary Destiny Points either way**, then choose to:
> - **switch** to the new card — you lose the previous Arcana's powers and adopt the new one's, or
> - **keep** your current Arcana — the new card's powers are discarded.

Each Major Arcana provides:

- **Meaning** — a symbolic summary.
- **Destiny Impact** — a modifier to the initial Destiny score.
- **Power** — a regularly usable ability (like a racial trait).
- **Vibration** — an optional temporary effect on a critical Destiny roll, equivalent to a spell (spell level matches the die size).

| Die | Spell level of effect |
| --- | --------------------- |
| d4  | 1 |
| d6  | 2 |
| d8  | 3 |
| d10 | 4 |
| d12 | 5 |

## Drawing in the whole tarot deck

On an **Arcane Awakening** (your Destiny Points reach 0 after a Natural 20), you draw from the **whole tarot deck** — all 78 cards, Major and Minor Arcana: [Random Tarot Card Generator](https://randomtarotcard.com/).

- **Minor Arcana** — gain **temporary Destiny Points equal to the card's value** (numbered cards give their number, Ace = 1; **heads — Page, Knight, Queen, King — give 10**), and a single **Brick**: not a power, but the chance to dream something into being. *(Full rule: [§5.1](fates-hand-mechanic.md#51-the-brick-dreaming-something-into-being).)*
- **Major Arcana** — **+1 to your maximum Destiny Score and 10 temporary Destiny Points** either way, then **switch** to the new card or **keep** your current one (see the Destiny System, §5).

## 0. The Fool
- **Meaning** — innocence, beginnings, carefreeness, freedom. Unlimited potential, stepping into the unknown without fear. Exploration without attachments, trust in chance.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, you may roll 2 Destiny dice at once, take the total of both as a bonus, and pay only the price of the highest.
- **Vibration** — luck effect (*Bless* or *Guidance* depending on level).

## I. The Magician
- **Meaning** — ingenuity, creativity, mastery of tools. The intellect shapes reality. The initiator of change through willpower.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per long rest, adjust the size of a Destiny die of a willing creature (d4 into d12 or vice-versa).
- **Vibration** — minor illusion effect (*Silent Image*).

## II. The High Priestess
- **Meaning** — hidden knowledge, intuition, mystery. Inner wisdom, revelation through patient study. Silence and secrets of the soul.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — roll a Destiny die in secret and store it; you may use the result before the end of the next long rest.
- **Vibration** — divination effect (*Detect Evil and Good*, *Augury*).

## III. The Empress
- **Meaning** — fertility, abundance, growth, prosperity. Support, harmony, generosity.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — recover an additional Destiny Point after a long rest if you have 2 or less.
- **Vibration** — healing or reinforcement (*Cure Wounds*, *Heroism*).

## IV. The Emperor
- **Meaning** — authority, order, stable power. Construction, organization, protection.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per long rest, sacrifice a die to obtain the highest possible bonus non-critical result.
- **Vibration** — defensive effect (*Mage Armor*, *Shield* minor effect).

## V. The Hierophant
- **Meaning** — tradition, teaching, spiritual guidance. Faith, rituals, sacred order.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per short rest, as a reaction within 30 feet, you may convert one of your Destiny dice into healing and add it to one individual's short rest dice (+ Constitution bonus), with no cost in Destiny Points.
- **Vibration** — sacred blessing (*Bless*, *Protection from Evil and Good*).

## VI. The Lovers
- **Meaning** — choice, harmony, relationships, moral dilemmas. Union, sacrifice, cooperation.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, as a reaction, roll a Destiny die to the benefit of one party member within 30 ft of you, while another (including you) suffers the cost.
- **Vibration** — social charm / inspiration (*Charm Person*).

## VII. The Chariot
- **Meaning** — victory, determination, triumph. Active control, moving toward success.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per long rest, you have advantage on a Destiny die. Choose either the lowest or the highest.
- **Vibration** — acceleration (*Longstrider*, *Expeditious Retreat*).

## VIII. Strength
- **Meaning** — courage, mastery of instincts, perseverance, moral and physical endurance.
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — the critical range of your Destiny dice is doubled (3 or 4 on a d4, 5 or 6 on a d6).
- **Vibration** — physical enhancement (*Enhance Ability* — Strength).

## IX. The Hermit
- **Meaning** — introspection, solitude, search for truth. Knowledge found in inner calm.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, you can store the result of your lowest available Destiny die and use it later.
- **Vibration** — clairvoyance / guidance (e.g. *Guidance*).

## X. Wheel of Fortune
- **Meaning** — change, unstable destiny, opportunity, unpredictability. Embracing the cycle of ups and downs.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — on the first Destiny roll of the day you choose either advantage or disadvantage, then a cycle is in place. During that same day, any individual (including you) within a 30-foot radius rolling a Destiny die rolls with advantage if you chose disadvantage, or the contrary.
- **Vibration** — random yet beneficial effect (e.g. advantage on the next roll).

## XI. Justice
- **Meaning** — fairness, responsibility, moral order. Evaluating, assuming responsibility, restoring balance.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — after a long rest, you may transfer up to 2 Destiny Points and 2 Destiny dice from the strongest to the weakest. The recipients may be willing or not. You may freely rule on ties, die or points. This may concern up to 4 individuals.
- **Vibration** — restore balance (e.g. *Lesser Restoration*).

## XII. The Hanged Man
- **Meaning** — sacrifice, letting go, new perspective. Renouncing to gain understanding.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — if you **invoke Chaos** on a natural 1, you do not lose but immediately regain 2 Destiny Points and just roll 1d6 on the **[Chaos table](chaos-tables.md)**.
- **Vibration** — neutralization (e.g. targeted *Slow*).

## XIII. Death
- **Meaning** — transformation, end of a cycle, rebirth. Radical change, not absolute finality.
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — you are immune to everything above 6 on the **[Chaos table](chaos-tables.md)**, and trigger an Awakening on a 12.
- **Vibration** — survival / minor necromancy effect (e.g. *False Life*).

## XIV. Temperance
- **Meaning** — moderation, harmony, compromise. Finding balance and the right measure.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, in a 30-foot radius, as a reaction you can roll a Destiny die that will act as a buffer limiting the Destiny cost.
- **Vibration** — purification (e.g. *Protection from Poison*).

## XV. The Devil
- **Meaning** — temptation, illusions, voluntary servitude. Power at the cost of freedom.
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — once per short rest, roll a Destiny die in advance if you have one. As a reaction or even in advance, you may give it to anyone within 30 feet. It will cost the recipient one Destiny Point to acquire it; in return you collect this benefit as a temporary Destiny Point, and your die is blocked (unusable by you) until it is used by the recipient or at the end of their next long rest. When the die is used by the recipient it acts as if it were their own; your die then resets and you may use it again.
  - If the recipient did not use your die by the end of their long rest, they lose it and it becomes usable by you again.
  - An "infernal Awakening" as a reaction to using your die may have strange consequences.
- **Vibration** — minor curse (e.g. *Bane*).

## XVI. The Tower
- **Meaning** — shock, collapse, brutal revelation. Destruction to rebuild on truth.
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — once per long rest, as a reaction, sacrifice all your Destiny Points to transform any successful roll into a critical failure within 30 feet. Ignore the Chaos Effect.
- **Vibration** — controlled destruction (e.g. *Thunderwave*).

## XVII. The Star
- **Meaning** — hope, inspiration, celestial guidance. A beacon in the night, trust in the future.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, take the average (rounded up) of a Destiny die instead of rolling.
- **Vibration** — minor inspiration / healing (e.g. *Healing Word*, *Guidance*).

## XVIII. The Moon
- **Meaning** — illusions, dreams, nocturnal mysteries. The unconscious mind, distorted perception.
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — except for a critical Fail / Success, the Destiny cost is reduced by 1.
- **Vibration** — illusion (e.g. *Silent Image*).

## XIX. The Sun
- **Meaning** — success, vitality, clarity, joy. Dispels shadows, brings optimism and truth.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per short rest, add +2 to the result of a Destiny die without any additional cost.
- **Vibration** — minor light and healing (e.g. *Light* + small heal).

## XX. Judgement
- **Meaning** — rebirth, higher calling, final reckoning. Weighing the past, offering redemption or judgment.
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — once per short rest you may spend a Destiny die, with no cost in Destiny Points, to replace any d20 roll by a 10.
- **Vibration** — moral command (e.g. *Command*).

## XXI. The World
- **Meaning** — accomplishment, wholeness, unity. The end of one cycle, opening to a new beginning.
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — after a long rest, restore one Destiny Point to group members excluding yourself. Roll a die of your choice:
  - 1d4 for 1 to 4 people
  - 1d6 for 5 to 6 people
  - 1d8 for 7 to 8 people
  - 1d10 for 9 to 10 people
  - 1d12 for 11 to 12 people

  You pay the price rolled by your die.

- **Vibration** — versatile beneficial effect (small global buff).
