# Feats

Fate's Hand modifications to the 2024 feats. Only the **changes** are listed — everything else works as printed.

## Skill feats

### [Observant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#Observant)
Also lets you add either your **Intelligence** or **Wisdom** modifier to **Survival**, **Delve** and **Vigilance** checks.

### [Keen Mind](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#KeenMind)
Also lets you add either your **Intelligence** or **Wisdom** modifier to **Insight** and **Investigation** checks.

### [Actor](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#Actor)
As written.

### Lucky
Additionally, **+2** to your **Destiny Score**.

## Combat feats

### [Spell Sniper](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789201-spell-sniper)
- Cast in melee.
- Increased range.
- **Critical range +1**.
- Cancels the chance of friendly spell-fire on allies in the melee.
- Cancels the rest.

### [Great Weapon Fighting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789148-great-weapon-fighting)
Adds your **proficiency bonus** to damage *(this bonus goes into "graze")*.

### [Heavy Weapon Mastery](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789149-great-weapon-master)
Adds your **proficiency bonus** to damage, provided you don't move this round *(stacks with **Great Weapon Fighting**)*. "Hew" remains.

### [Sharpshooter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789187-sharpshooter)
- Adds your **proficiency bonus** to damage.
- Fire in melee without **disadvantage**.
- Increased range.
- Cancels the chance of friendly ranged fire on allies in the melee.
- Cancels the rest.

### Dual Wielder
*Reminder — this is real 2024 rules, not homebrew.*

- Lets you swap or draw an extra weapon on your turn (an extra weapon interaction).
- Lets you make the **bonus-action** attack even without an off-hand weapon — e.g. while holding a **shield**.
- With **Nick** (which folds the off-hand light-weapon attack into your **Attack** action, freeing your bonus action) and **Vex**, this yields a **third attack with a shield up**.

> [!note] The 3-attack shield combo
> Full walkthrough in `8. Other rules/Shield dual wield 3 attacks.md`: **Vex** → **Nick** → **Dual Wielder**, juggling weapons with object interactions.

## Command feats

### Battlefield Tactician
- **+1** to **INT**, **WIS** or **CHA**.
- The **Help**, **Study** and tactical-order actions take a **bonus action** instead of an action.

### Battlefield Master
*Prerequisite: **Battlefield Tactician** — improves it.*

- Enables **mass Leadership**: you can lead other leaders, but only those who are themselves **Battlefield Tacticians** or have **Expertise** in the directed action; you must be able to see each other.
- The check is **DC 12**, affecting one additional group per increment of 2. On a success, each sub-leader gains a **synergy bonus** on their **Leadership** rolls, applied to their group for one minute. See the Leadership rules in `4. Skills`.
