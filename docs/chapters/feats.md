# Feats — Fate's Hand modifications

*(new)* marks a Fate's Hand addition to the 2024 rules.

**[Observant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#Observant)** — also lets you add either your **Intelligence** or **Wisdom** modifier to **Survival**, **Delve** and **Vigilance** checks.

**[Keen Mind](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#KeenMind)** — also lets you add either your **Intelligence** or **Wisdom** modifier to **Insight** and **Investigation** checks.

**[Actor](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#Actor)** — as written.

**Lucky** — additionally, **+2** to your **Destiny Score**.

**[Spell Sniper](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789201-spell-sniper)**
- Cast in melee.
- Increased range.
- **Critical range +1** *(new)*.
- Cancels the chance of friendly spell-fire on allies in the melee.
- Cancels the rest.

**[Great Weapon Fighting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789148-great-weapon-fighting)**
- Adds your **proficiency bonus** to damage *(this bonus goes into "graze")*.

**[Heavy Weapon Mastery](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789149-great-weapon-master)**
- Adds your **proficiency bonus** to damage, provided you don't move this round *(stacks with **Great Weapon Fighting**)*.
- "Hew" remains.

**Dual Wielder** *(RAW reminder — real 2024 rules, not homebrew)*
- Lets you swap or draw an extra weapon on your turn (an extra weapon interaction).
- Lets you make the **bonus-action** attack even without an off-hand weapon — e.g. while holding a **shield**.
- With **Nick** — which folds the off-hand light-weapon attack into your **Attack** action, freeing your bonus action — and **Vex**, this yields a third attack with a shield up. Full walkthrough: `8. Other rules/Shield dual wield 3 attacks.md`.

**[Sharpshooter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789187-sharpshooter)**
- Adds your **proficiency bonus** to damage *(new)*.
- Fire in melee without **disadvantage**.
- Increased range.
- Cancels the chance of friendly ranged fire on allies in the melee.
- Cancels the rest.

**Battlefield Tactician**
- **+1** to **INT**, **WIS** or **CHA**.
- The **Help**, **Study** and tactical-order actions take a **bonus action** instead of an action.

**Battlefield Master** *(prerequisite: **Battlefield Tactician** — improves it)*
- Enables mass **Leadership**: you can lead other leaders, but only those who are themselves **Battlefield Tacticians** or have **Expertise** in the directed action; you must be able to see each other.
- The check is **DC 12**, affecting one additional group per increment of 2. On a success, each sub-leader gains a **synergy bonus** on their **Leadership** rolls, applied to their group for one minute. See the Leadership rules in `4. Skills`.
