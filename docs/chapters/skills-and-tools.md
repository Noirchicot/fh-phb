# Fate's Hand 5+ — Skills & Tools: Player Guide

A plain-language guide to how skill points work and what every skill and tool does.

---

## 1. The quick version

- Fate's Hand replaces the 17-skill list with **26 skills**, and folds tools into the same point pool.
- You spend points to raise skills and tools to one of four tiers.
- **Tiers & cost:** None 0 · Half 1 pt · Proficient 2 pts · Expertise 4 pts.
- *(Half = half your proficiency bonus, rounded down. Expertise = double proficiency.)*

---

## 2. Building at character creation

### Your starting pool

Your pool = Class base + Background (6) + Race bonus + Feats (e.g. Skilled +6).

**Class base** (the "+2 breadth" bonus is already baked in — don't add it again):

| Class | Base |
|---|---|
| Bard, Ranger | **10** |
| Rogue | **12** |
| Everyone else (Barbarian, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard, **Artificer**) | **8** |

**Race bonus** — only three species give points:

| Species | At creation | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Human, Half-Elf | +2 | +2 at levels **3** and **6** |
| Dwarf | +2 *(tools only)* | — |
| All others (Elf included) | 0 | — |

### Rules at creation
- At least **1 point** must go into a tool.
- Languages are free at creation — they cost nothing.
- **Expertise** at creation is reserved for the four **skill classes** (Bard, Ranger, Rogue, Wizard); everyone else can buy it from level 4.
- The breadth points are free to place — no forced spread. The one standing rule is the conservation rule (§3).

> *Examples:* Talia (Human Ranger, lvl 1) = 10 + 6 + 2 = **18 pts** · Kai (Fighter, lvl 1) = 8 + 6 = **14 pts** · a Human Rogue lvl 1 = 12 + 6 + 2 = **20 pts**.

## 3. Levelling up & evolution

- **+2 points every 4 levels** for all classes (levels **4** and **8**).
- **Skill classes** (Bard, Ranger, Rogue, Wizard) get **extra +2 at levels 3, 5 and 7** — so they bump at 3, 4, 5, 7, 8.
- **Buying languages later:** after creation, a new language costs **1 point**.
- **Moving a point:** each time you gain points (a level-up), you may **shift a point from one skill/tool to another** — as long as you respect the conservation rule below.

> [!note] Conservation rule — the only hard constraint
> You must keep at least **Half** on every native skill and every native tool your class, background or species granted. You may drop a native from **Proficient** to **Half** to free a point — but never below **Half**.

### Multiclassing
- Your **base** comes from your **starting class only** (no second base).
- **Level bonuses stack per class**, by the thresholds you actually reach in each.
  > *Akbal (Warlock 5 / Monk 4): base 8 + background 6 + Warlock@4 (+2) + Monk@4 (+2) = **18 pts**.*

## 6. Key reminders

- **Synergies:** a related skill/tool can grant advantage or a bonus when it makes sense — e.g. *Stealth + Hunting* (staying hidden), *Animal Handling + Mount* (riding), *Hunting + Survival* (the wild). Synergy DC tier 1 = **16** (scale 10 / 16 / 22).
- **Riding** is rolled under the relevant **Mount** tool; **Animal Handling** adds a synergy bonus, it isn't the ride roll itself.
- **Knowledge by creature type** and the **Study** action are detailed in D&G 5+ Revisited Skills.md (section "Connaissance & actions de combat").
- **Leadership** has its own full ruleset (cost, DC 12 + targets, combat reactions) — see the same doc, or the ⓘ popup in the builder.

> Use the **builder** ([the skill builder](../builder.md)) to compute your pool, place points, respect the conservation rule, and export everything to D&D Beyond.

## 7. Detailed notes on complex skills

*The linked skills in the tables above point here. Full rules live in Skill chapters/D&G 5+ Revisited Skills.md ("Connaissance & actions de combat").*

### The Perception split (Delve, Survival, Vigilance)
Old D&D Perception is gone, split into three context-based skills so the right ability and training matter:

- Delve (WIS) — perceiving a built / urban environment: ruins, dungeons, architecture, hidden passages, reading the terrain you move through.
- Survival (WIS) — the same, but in a natural environment: wilderness, weather, terrain, tracks.
- Vigilance (WIS) — perceiving immediate danger: noticing an ambush, a threat, something wrong right now.

### Animal Handling & riding
Animal Handling covers comprehending, training and interacting with beasts (soothe, calm, command, raise).

Riding a mount is not an Animal Handling check — it is rolled under the relevant Mount tool (Air / Land / Water). Animal Handling adds a synergy bonus to the ride when relevant; it doesn't replace the Mount roll.

### Hunting
Covers tracking quarry, skinning / butchering game, and camouflaging while still (a blind, a hide, lying in wait).

- **Synergy with Stealth (stillness only):** as you move from one cover to the next you roll Stealth at DC 14 — moving gives no bonus, it stays 14. The moment you reach cover and stop moving, Hunting grants its +2 camouflage synergy (while you hold still). Stealth on its own never gets the +2 while moving.
- **Synergy with Survival / Nature:** in the wild (reading sign, knowing the prey).

### Tactics & the Study action
- **Tactics** (INT) = strategic planning, ambushes, and **assessing the power of a force** (numbers, discipline, threat level).
- **Study action**: spend an action to *Study* something and convert knowledge into usable intel —
  - *Tactics* → the **strength of an opposing force**.
  - *Knowledge-by-type skill* (below) → a specific creature's **abilities, resistances and weak points**.

### Knowledge by creature type
To acquire knowledge on creatures through a Study action:

- **Arcana** — Aberrations, Elementals
- **Religion** — Celestials, Fiends, Undead
- **Investigation** — Constructs, Monstrosities
- **Medicine** — Giants, Humanoids, Undead
- **Nature** — Oozes, Plants, Beasts
- **History** — Humanoids, Dragons, Fey

### Leadership
A CHA skill to coordinate and command allies.

**Prerequisite:** the leader and the target must each be at least Half-proficient in the skill, attack, or spell being directed — otherwise the order has no effect.

- **Cost:** a Utilize action — a bonus action if you have the [Battlefield Tactician](feats.md#battlefield-tactician) feat, or have Expertise in the required skill.
- **Destiny:** affecting more than one person costs 1 Destiny Point per use.
- **Check:** DC 12, all targets within a 30-ft radius.

You affect more people the higher you beat the DC (+1 per 2 points):

| Leadership result | People affected |
|:---:|:---:|
| 12 | 1 |
| 14 | 2 |
| 16 | 3 |
| 18 | 4 |
| each +2 | +1 |

#### Outside combat (skills)
A success grants +2 to everyone affected.

#### In combat — on the leader's turn
Affected allies gain a reaction to perform the directed action. Both parties must be competent:

| Directed action | Condition |
|---|---|
| Attack | only if the weapon is proficient for both (else ineffective) |
| Spell | only if both know the same spell |
| Skill | allowed, but without the +2 |

#### Mass effect — the Battlefield Master feat
[Battlefield Master](feats.md#battlefield-master) may improve sub-leaders who are themselves [Battlefield Tacticians](feats.md#battlefield-tactician) or have Expertise in the required course of action; they must be able to see each other. The check is DC 12, affecting one additional group per increment of 2 (12 = 1 group, 14 = 2, 16 = 3…).

On a success, each sub-leader gains a synergy bonus on their Leadership rolls, applied to their group for one minute.

### The Garrot
A finesse weapon (DEX) that improves [Strangling](battlefield.md#strangle-new) (see [Battlefield Rules](battlefield.md)). Bought through the skill pool for 2 points; the Assassin gets it free at level 3.

- Deals no damage (unless you have the Grappler feat) — only exhaustion.
- Usable only against a surprised target.
- You don't take the −2 Strangling penalty to hit.
- **Escape DC:** 10 + STR + PB (DEX for Rogues) — higher than a bare-handed strangle (8 + STR + PB), so the garrot makes the hold harder to break.
