# XII. The Hanged Man

![The Hanged Man — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XII.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — sacrifice, letting go, new perspective. Renouncing to gain understanding.
- **Signature Ability** — Dexterity
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — if you invoke **Chaos** on a natural 1, you do not lose but immediately regain 2 Destiny Points and just roll 1d6 on the **[Chaos table](../chaos-tables.md)**.

### Vibrations of The Hanged Man { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Inverted Sight** | swap your initiative with a willing creature's for the encounter |
| 2 | **Letting Go** | *Feather Fall* |
| 3 | **Martyr's Trade** | 1 min: when an ally within 30 ft takes damage, your reaction lets you take it instead, reduced by 1d6 |
| 4 | **Rope Unseen** | one creature is restrained and suspended 5 ft off the ground for 1 minute, no damage (WIS save, save ends at turn end) |
| 5 | **Eye Unbound** | *Arcane Eye* |
| 6 | **The Price of Knowing** | *Contact Other Plane* |
