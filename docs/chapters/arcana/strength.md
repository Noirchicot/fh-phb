# VIII. Strength

![Strength — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/VIII.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — courage, mastery of instincts, perseverance, moral and physical endurance.
- **Signature Ability** — Strength
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — the critical range of your Destiny dice is doubled (3 or 4 on a d4, 5 or 6 on a d6).

#### Vibrations of Strength { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Steady Nerve** | advantage on one Strength check or saving throw |
| 2 | **Lion's Heart** | *Heroism* |
| 3 | **Tamer's Grip** | *Enhance Ability* (Strength) |
| 4 | **Endurance of the Mountain** | 1 min: when you take damage, use your reaction to reduce it by 1d12 + CON |
| 5 | **The Lion Tamed** | *Polymorph* (beast form) |
| 6 | **Chains Undone** | 1 min: immune to grappled, restrained, paralyzed and stunned; advantage on STR and CON saves |
