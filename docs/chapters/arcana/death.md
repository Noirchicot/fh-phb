# XIII. Death

![Death — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XIII.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — transformation, end of a cycle, rebirth. Radical change, not absolute finality.
- **Signature Ability** — Constitution
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — you are immune to everything above 6 on the **[Chaos table](../chaos-tables.md)**, and trigger an Awakening on a 12.

#### Vibrations of Death { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Last Breath Sensed** | as *Spare the Dying*, and you learn how close to death the target is |
| 2 | **Borrowed Time** | *False Life* |
| 3 | **Grave Chill** | one creature: 3d6 necrotic and its speed is halved for 1 round (CON save) |
| 4 | **End of a Cycle** | *Speak with Dead* |
| 5 | **Harvest of Souls** | one creature: 9d6 necrotic (CON save half) |
| 6 | **Rebirth** | *Raise Dead* |
