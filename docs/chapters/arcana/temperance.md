# XIV. Temperance

![Temperance — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XIV.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — moderation, harmony, compromise. Finding balance and the right measure.
- **Signature Ability** — Constitution
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, in a 30-foot radius, as a reaction you can roll a Destiny die that will act as a buffer limiting the Destiny cost.

#### Vibrations of Temperance { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Measured Breath** | advantage on one Constitution saving throw |
| 2 | **Purity of the Cup** | *Purify Food and Drink* |
| 3 | **Balanced Humors** | 1 h: resistance to poison damage and advantage on saves against poison and disease |
| 4 | **Even Keel** | 1 min: you can gain neither advantage nor disadvantage, and critical hits against you become normal hits |
| 5 | **Waters Commanded** | *Control Water* |
| 6 | **The Unbreached Measure** | *Antilife Shell* |
