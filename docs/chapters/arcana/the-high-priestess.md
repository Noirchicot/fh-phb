# II. The High Priestess

![The High Priestess — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/II.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — hidden knowledge, intuition, mystery. Inner wisdom, revelation through patient study. Silence and secrets of the soul.
- **Signature Ability** — Wisdom
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — roll a Destiny die in secret and store it; you may use the result before the end of the next long rest.

#### Vibrations of The High Priestess { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |

| 2 | **Third Eye** | 1 h: tremorsense 30 ft |

| 5 | **Pierce the Veil** | *Divination* |
