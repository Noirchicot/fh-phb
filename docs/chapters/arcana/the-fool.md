# 0. The Fool

![The Fool — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/0.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — innocence, beginnings, carefreeness, freedom. Unlimited potential, stepping into the unknown without fear. Exploration without attachments, trust in chance.
- **Signature Ability** — Dexterity
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, you may roll 2 Destiny dice at once, take the total of both as a bonus, and pay only the price of the highest.

#### Vibrations of The Fool { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Fool's Step** | reroll one failed ability check; take the new roll |
| 2 | **Tumbler's Grace** | 1 min: advantage on DEX saves and you ignore falling damage from 20 ft or less |
| 3 | **Vagabond's Stride** | 1 h: +10 ft speed and spider climb |
| 4 | **Leap of Faith** | *Blink* |
| 5 | **Unbound Road** | *Freedom of Movement* |
| 6 | **Step Beyond** | *Passwall* |
