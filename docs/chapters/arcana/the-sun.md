# XIX. The Sun

![The Sun — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XIX.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — success, vitality, clarity, joy. Dispels shadows, brings optimism and truth.
- **Signature Ability** — Charisma
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per short rest, add +2 to the result of a Destiny die without any additional cost.

#### Vibrations of The Sun { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Ray of Morning** | as *Light*, and one creature in its glow gains 1d4 temporary HP |
| 2 | **All Made Plain** | *Faerie Fire* |
| 3 | **Solar Lash** | one creature: 5d6 radiant (DEX save half) |
| 4 | **Cleansing Light** | *Daylight* |
| 5 | **Corona** | creatures in a 10-ft emanation around you: 9d6 fire and radiant (DEX save half) |
| 6 | **Zenith of Life** | *Greater Restoration* |
