# X. Wheel of Fortune

![Wheel of Fortune — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/X.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — change, unstable destiny, opportunity, unpredictability. Embracing the cycle of ups and downs.
- **Signature Ability** — Charisma
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — on the first Destiny roll of the day you choose either advantage or disadvantage, then a cycle is in place. During that same day, any individual (including you) within a 30-foot radius rolling a Destiny die rolls with advantage if you chose disadvantage, or the contrary.

#### Vibrations of Wheel of Fortune { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Spin of Fate** | reroll one d20 just rolled by any creature you can see |
| 2 | **Turn of the Wheel** | *Bless* **or** *Bane* — choose when released |
| 3 | **Rising Spoke** | 1 min: at the start of each of your turns roll 1d6 — even: advantage on your first d20 test; odd: the first attack against you this round has disadvantage |
| 4 | **Fortune Reversed** | *Bestow Curse* **or** *Remove Curse* — choose when released |
| 5 | **The Great Cycle** | *Confusion* |
| 6 | **Fate Rewritten** | within the next minute, when a creature within 60 ft succeeds or fails a d20 test, you may invert the result — a success becomes a failure or the reverse (once) |
