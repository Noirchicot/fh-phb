# IX. The Hermit

![The Hermit — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/IX.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — introspection, solitude, search for truth. Knowledge found in inner calm.
- **Signature Ability** — Intelligence
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, you can store the result of your lowest available Destiny die and use it later.

### Vibrations of The Hermit { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Quiet Mind** | advantage on one check made with no ally within 30 ft |
| 2 | **Lantern of the Unseen** | 1 h: you see normally in darkness, magical or not, to 120 ft |
| 3 | **See Truly** | *See Invisibility* |
| 4 | **Solitude's Shield** | *Nondetection* |
| 5 | **Hermit's Cell** | *Resilient Sphere* |
| 6 | **Eye of the Summit** | truesight 120 ft for 10 minutes |
