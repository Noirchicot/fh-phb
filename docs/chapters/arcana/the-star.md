# XVII. The Star

![The Star — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XVII.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — hope, inspiration, celestial guidance. A beacon in the night, trust in the future.
- **Signature Ability** — Wisdom
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, take the average (rounded up) of a Destiny die instead of rolling.

### Vibrations of The Star { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Starlit Spark** | add 1d4 to one ability check |
| 2 | **Gleam of Hope** | *Healing Word* |
| 3 | **Vigil of the Star** | 1 min: one creature has advantage on all saving throws |
| 4 | **Beacon Ascendant** | *Beacon of Hope* |
| 5 | **The Pole Star** | 1 h: allies who can see you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened |
| 6 | **Dream of Morning** | *Dream* |
