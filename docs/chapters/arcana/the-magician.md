# I. The Magician

![The Magician — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/I.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — ingenuity, creativity, mastery of tools. The intellect shapes reality. The initiator of change through willpower.
- **Signature Ability** — Intelligence
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per long rest, adjust the size of a Destiny die of a willing creature (d4 into d12 or vice-versa).

#### Vibrations of The Magician { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Sleight of Will** | one *Prestidigitation* effect, lasting up to 1 hour |
| 2 | **Tool of Will** | 1 h: a weapon you touch is a +1 weapon |
| 3 | **Threefold Hand** | 1 min: a spectral hand manipulates at 30 ft and can strike as a bonus action, 1d6 force |
| 4 | **Will into Form** | *Major Image* |
| 5 | **Master's Fabrication** | *Fabricate* |
| 6 | **Reality's Apprentice** | *Creation* |
