# VI. The Lovers

![The Lovers — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/VI.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — choice, harmony, relationships, moral dilemmas. Union, sacrifice, cooperation.
- **Signature Ability** — Charisma
- **Destiny Impact** — +2
- **Power** — once per long rest, as a reaction, roll a Destiny die to the benefit of one party member within 30 ft of you, while another (including you) suffers the cost.

### Vibrations of The Lovers { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Heart's Glance** | advantage on one Charisma (Persuasion) check |
| 2 | **Entwined Hearts** | *Charm Person* |
| 3 | **Shared Burden** | 1 h: bond two willing creatures — when one takes damage, the other may take half of it instead |
| 4 | **Whispered Vow** | *Sending* |
| 5 | **Union's Shield** | 1 h: bond with one willing creature — while within 5 ft of each other, you both gain +1 AC and advantage on saving throws |
| 6 | **Twin Souls** | *Telepathic Bond* |
