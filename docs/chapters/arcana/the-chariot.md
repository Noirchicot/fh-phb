# VII. The Chariot

![The Chariot — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/VII.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — victory, determination, triumph. Active control, moving toward success.
- **Signature Ability** — Strength
- **Destiny Impact** — +1
- **Power** — once per long rest, you have advantage on a Destiny die. Choose either the lowest or the highest.

#### Vibrations of The Chariot { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Charge Onward** | +10 ft speed for 1 minute |
| 2 | **Jaunt of the Charioteer** | as a bonus action, teleport up to 30 ft to a space you can see |
| 3 | **Trample** | move up to 30 ft in a line; each creature in your path: 3d6 bludgeoning and knocked prone (STR save) |
| 4 | **Unstoppable Course** | *Haste* |
| 5 | **Triumphal Surge** | *Dimension Door* |
| 6 | **Master of the Field** | *Telekinesis* |
