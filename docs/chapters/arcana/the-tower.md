# XVI. The Tower

![The Tower — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XVI.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — shock, collapse, brutal revelation. Destruction to rebuild on truth.
- **Signature Ability** — Strength
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — once per long rest, as a reaction, sacrifice all your Destiny Points to transform any successful roll into a critical failure within 30 feet. Ignore the Chaos Effect.

### Vibrations of The Tower { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **First Crack** | 1d6 thunder damage to one target, audible 300 ft away |
| 2 | **Sudden Rupture** | *Thunderwave* |
| 3 | **Shattering Peal** | *Shatter* |
| 4 | **Bolt from Heaven** | *Lightning Bolt* |
| 5 | **Sky's Collapse** | 7d6 thunder, 20-ft-radius sphere (DEX save half) *(5 pp = 7d6 @A)* |
| 6 | **The Tower Falls** | 9d6 thunder and fire, 20-ft-radius sphere (DEX save half) *(6 pp = 9d6 @A)* |
