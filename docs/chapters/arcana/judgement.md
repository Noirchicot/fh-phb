# XX. Judgement

![Judgement — Fate's Hand tarot](../../assets/img/tarot/major/XX.jpg){ .fh-card-illus }

- **Meaning** — rebirth, higher calling, final reckoning. Weighing the past, offering redemption or judgment.
- **Signature Ability** — Wisdom
- **Destiny Impact** — +0
- **Power** — once per short rest you may spend a Destiny die, with no cost in Destiny Points, to replace any d20 roll by a 10.

### Vibrations of Judgement { .fh-clear }

| Rank (vp) | Vibration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Herald's Note** | advantage on one Charisma (Intimidation) check |
| 2 | **First Trumpet** | *Command* |
| 3 | **Voice That Wakes** | *Zone of Truth* |
| 4 | **Called to Account** | *Revivify* |
| 5 | **The Reckoning** | one creature: 6d6 radiant and frightened for 1 round (WIS save) |
| 6 | **The Court Eternal** | 1 min: 30-ft aura — undead and fiends inside have disadvantage on every d20 test, and your allies inside cannot be frightened |
