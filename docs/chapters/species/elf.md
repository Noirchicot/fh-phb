# [Elf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751437-elf)

![Elf](../../assets/img/species-elf.jpg){ .fh-portrait }

Elves are the only people of Nymedes who do not die of time. What they die of instead is everything else — a blade, a fall, a war — and so the elven number only ever falls. No elf has been born of two elves in living memory, nor in any memory an archive holds: elven unions bear nothing. Only a human or an Araag quickens an elven womb, and what comes of it is **Elestu** — a child who will grow old, and whose own children will be something else again. Every elf alive today was alive in the First Age.

That arithmetic explains the rest of them. A census-keeper of Huitzlika put it plainly: an archivist may serve three lifetimes and never see one. In the Third Age you are far likelier to meet an elf's great-grandchild in the university quarter of Theymoron — the Elestu keep the Moonkeeper circles and the lecture halls — than to meet the elf. The few who still walk the roads travel as guests who never quite arrive: courteous, unhurried, and privately certain that the kingdom hosting them will not outlast the conversation.

Age does not bend them; it empties them. The oldest withdraw by degrees, and there are two ways it goes. Some slip into a sleep measured in centuries, laid in halls their descendants no longer remember how to open. Others go into a quiet madness no healer's art has ever touched — lucid, gentle, and no longer addressed to anyone present. An elven household will tell you which of its elders is merely absent and which is gone, and will not explain how it knows.

They are wanted dead by people who would never say so. A soulgem cut from a dying elf is the finest thing a soulforger will ever hold — denser, older and stranger than any other, and worth what a small city is worth. The trade is illegal everywhere and conducted everywhere, and it is the most reliable way an elf still ends.

The old traditions have an explanation, and they hold it lightly. They name the elves the children of a sleeping god — one who is said to have bought their immortality with his own, breaking himself into as many pieces as there were elves to carry them. In that telling an elf's soulgem is not merely fine: it holds a splinter of a god's memory, sealed away since the First Age and still faintly awake. Scholars disagree, at length, and have for eleven centuries. Elves themselves rarely take a position; those who do tend to be the ones who dream badly.

Whatever the truth of it, something in the elven line answers when fate is asked directly — which is why an elf who leans on the Hand leans further than anyone else can.

**Traits**

**Creature type** Humanoid · **Size** Medium (about 5–6 feet tall) · **Speed** 30 feet

- **Darkvision** — 60 feet.
- **Elven Lineage** — choose a lineage from the Elven Lineages table; you gain its level 1 benefit. At character levels 3 and 5 you learn the listed spell, always prepared, castable once without a slot per Long Rest, or with any slot of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this trait (chosen with the lineage).
- **Fey Ancestry** — Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
- **Keen Senses** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — 2 [skill points](../skills-and-tools.md), spendable only on **Survival**, **Delve** or **Vigilance**, at the normal cost: half on two of the three, or full on a single one. *(The base game grants one proficiency among Insight, Perception or Survival.)*
- **Trance** — you don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours of trancelike meditation, during which you retain consciousness.
- **Destiny** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — Base 2. **Splinter of Anon** *(elf chosen)*: +2 to your Destiny pool.

**Elven Lineages**

| Lineage | Level 1 | Level 3 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drow** | Darkvision increases to 120 feet; you know *Dancing Lights* | *Faerie Fire* | *Darkness* |
| **High Elf** | You know *Prestidigitation*; on a Long Rest you may swap it for another Wizard cantrip | *Detect Magic* | *Misty Step* |
| **Wood Elf** | Your Speed increases to 35 feet; you know *Druidcraft* | *Longstrider* | *Pass without Trace* |
