# lestu

![Elestu](../../assets/img/species-elestu.jpg){ .fh-portrait }

Children of scattered pairings between elves and humans — or elves and Araags — the Elestu are what an elven union actually bears: too mortal for the elves, too strange for everyone else. In Theymoron they gravitate to the university quarter and the Moonkeeper circles, and built their own quiet place between both worlds. Most end up bards, scholars, or both at once.

They exist because elves cannot have elven children. Only a human or an Araag quickens an elven womb, and what is born is Elestu — long-lived, not deathless, and carrying enough of the elven inheritance to be recognised on sight and not enough to be claimed. Elven households are unfailingly kind to them and do not treat them as heirs. Elestu have made a great deal of poetry out of this and very little complaint.

Theymoron is where they concentrated, and it is one in forty of the city — enough to fill the lecture halls of the university quarter and to keep the Moonkeeper circles running without ever being numerous enough to be a faction. It suits them. An Elestu in a room tends to be the person who knows what the argument is actually about, has read the source everyone is quoting badly, and would rather not chair the meeting. Their neighbours read this as modesty. It is closer to a professional preference for staying where the records are.

They take the trance from their elven side, and the appetite from the other: four hours of open-eyed meditation instead of sleep, and a working day long enough to embarrass a human colleague. Elestu learn faster than anyone has a good explanation for. The polite theory in the university quarter is that a mind kept awake for twenty hours a day simply accumulates more; the impolite one, whispered in the Moonkeeper circles, is that whatever the elves are carrying passes down diluted and restless, and shows up as curiosity because there is nothing else left for it to be.

Elestu do not usually argue with either theory. They write both down, note who said what, and go back to work.

**Traits**

**Creature type** Humanoid · **Size** Medium · **Speed** 30 feet

- **Darkvision** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — 60 feet.
- **Fey Ancestry** — Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
- **Trance** — you don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours of trancelike meditation, during which you retain consciousness.
- **Keen Senses** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — 2 [skill points](../skills-and-tools.md), spendable only on **Survival**, **Delve** or **Vigilance**, at the normal cost: half on two of the three, or full on a single one. *(The base game grants one proficiency among Insight, Perception or Survival.)*
- **Fast Learner** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — +2 skill points at levels 1, 3 and 6.
- **Destiny** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — Base 2.
