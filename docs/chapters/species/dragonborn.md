# Dragonborn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751435-dragonborn)

![Dragonborn](../../assets/img/species-dragonborn.jpg){ .fh-portrait }

Dragon-blooded wanderers whose scales mark them from birth as heirs to something older than any empire on Nymedes. Most trace their line to no living dragon at all — only to the breath that still wakes in their chest when they are cornered.

That is the polite version, and dragonborn tell it themselves. The blunter one is that the ancestry is an idea more reliably than it is a genealogy. Scale runs jet to gold across the old dragon families and settles nothing about a stranger's temper; two dragonborn of the same colour, met in the same tavern, will disagree about what the colour means. What they will not disagree about is the breath. It arrives before the argument is finished, and it is the same weapon their ancestor carried.

Nymedes gives the claim more weight than most worlds would, because here the dragons are real, few, and near. Seven red dragons still live over Theymoron, and their Riders are the reason that kingdom has never paid tribute. Four more died in the thirty years of war, and every child in the terraces knows their names, their battles and where they fell. A dragonborn walking into Theymoron is walking into a city that takes his ancestry literally — sometimes as an honour, more often as a question about which side his great-grandfather was on.

Elsewhere he is simply large, warm to the touch, and hard to seat at a crowded table. Dragonborn drift: through the roads and the wars, says the census-keeper, which is his way of saying they are found everywhere and settled nowhere. They tend to arrive where something is being defended and to leave once it is. Scholars in Huitzlika call this rootlessness; dragonborn call it not having been invited to stay, and the two accounts fit the same facts.

What is not in doubt is the posture. A dragonborn does not apologise for the room he takes up, and does not read the request to do so as politeness. Push either trait — the pride or the breath — and you find out fast which one answers first.

**Traits**

**Creature type** Humanoid · **Size** Medium (about 5–7 feet tall) · **Speed** 30 feet

- **Darkvision** — 60 feet.
- **Draconic Ancestry** — your lineage stems from a dragon progenitor. Choose the kind of dragon from the Draconic Ancestors table; your choice sets your **Breath Weapon** and **Damage Resistance**, and your appearance.
- **Breath Weapon** — in place of one attack, exhale magical energy in a 15-foot Cone or a 30-foot Line, 5 feet wide. Dexterity save (DC 8 + Constitution modifier + 2, rising by 1 at levels 5, 9, 13 and 17); 1d10 damage of your ancestry's type, half on a success. It rises to 2d10 at level 5, 3d10 at 11 and 4d10 at 17. 2 uses (+1 at levels 5, 9, 13 and 17), regained on a Long Rest.
- **Damage Resistance** — to the damage type set by your Draconic Ancestry.
- **Draconic Flight** — at level 5, sprout spectral wings as a Bonus Action for 10 minutes: a Fly Speed equal to your Speed.
- **Destiny** <span class="fh-tag">FH</span> — Base 2.
