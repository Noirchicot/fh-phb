# Identity

**Step 1, and nothing here costs a point.** Before a species grants you anything, before a
class binds a skill, before an Inheritance hands you two languages — there is what is simply
yours. Fate's Hand asks for three things, and none of them is rolled.

## Your name

A name is not decoration: it is the first thing another character learns about you, and it
says where you come from before you open your mouth.

**Names follow peoples, not species.** Two Elestu raised a thousand miles apart do not name
their children alike, and an Araag brought up among Hoddon carries a Hoddon name. Choose the
name of the culture that **raised** you, which is not always the one that bore you.

📌 The same logic runs through the languages you speak: **a tongue carries the name of its
people and nothing else** — there is no *Elvish* in Fate's Hand, only *Elf*. See
**Inheritance**.

## Your alignment

Two axes, and the SRD's own words for them are in the **Rules Glossary**. Fate's Hand changes
nothing about what alignment *is*.

What Fate's Hand changes is **how much it weighs**. Your standing in the world is tracked by
your **Destiny Score** and by glory and damnation, which move with what you do — not by two
letters written at creation. **Alignment is a declaration of intent, not a cage**: it says how
you mean to act, and the table finds out whether you meant it.

→ **The Fate's Hand Mechanic** for Destiny, glory and damnation.

## Your gender

**Yours to say, and it carries no mechanical weight whatsoever** — man, woman, or something
your people have a word for and ours does not. No score, no bonus, no restriction: nothing in
Fate's Hand reads this field.

⛔ **No species, class, feat or item in Fate's Hand is gated on gender**, and none will be.
