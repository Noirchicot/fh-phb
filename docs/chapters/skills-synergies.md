## Overview

At levels **1–4**, the base **DC** for most challenging skill checks is **16**, rising with tier at higher levels (DM's tiered table). This higher DC keeps challenges interesting while rewarding creative teamwork. You can gain a synergy bonus in three ways:

| Synergy | Granted when… |
| --- | --- |
| **Individual** | two of your own skills or tools support each other in the situation. |
| **Cooperative** | an ally who is **at least Novice** in the skill (or their tool) meaningfully assists. |
| **Situational** | the environment or story context directly favors you. |

> [!note] One synergy or two
> Take **one** synergy bonus for **+2**, or **two** synergy bonuses for **Advantage** (instead of a bigger flat bonus). You **cannot** stack synergy with another source of Advantage — if you already have Advantage, you gain no extra +2 and no "super-Advantage."

## How Much You Get

| Synergy factors | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| One | **+2** (effectively turning a DC 16 into a 14) |
| Two distinct | **Advantage** (not a flat +4) |
| You already have Advantage | no synergy applies |

## Examples

- **Individual** — a scout uses **Survival** to read the terrain while **Investigation** picks out the safest path: the two skills reinforce each other for **+2**.
- **Cooperative** — a rogue picks a lock (DC 16) while an ally **at least Novice** in Thieves' Tools steadies and guides them: **+2** to the check.
- **Situational** — a bard negotiates peace (DC 16) in a calm, neutral inn over warm drinks: the relaxed atmosphere grants **+2**.
- **Two factors → Advantage** — a ranger scales a cliff (DC 16) while a druid clears loose rocks (Situational) *and* a fighter holds a rope (Cooperative): the ranger climbs with **Advantage**.

## Tips for Players

- **Ask first.** Before rolling, explain narratively how an ally or the environment is helping. If the GM agrees, you get your **+2** or **Advantage**.
- **Stay creative.** Small details — cleaning a surface before forging a seal, steadying a hand — can grant a Situational bonus if they make a tangible in-game difference.
- **One vs. two.** One factor = **+2**; two distinct factors = **Advantage**. No triple-stacking, and no synergy if you already have Advantage.
