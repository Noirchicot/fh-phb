# Synergy Rules

## Overview
At levels **1–4**, the base **DC** for most challenging skill checks is **16** in our game (full tiered DC table in the Revisited doc). Characters can gain synergy bonuses in three ways:

1. **Individual Synergy** — two of your own skills/tools support each other in the situation.
2. **Cooperative Synergy** — another proficient ally (or their tool) assists.
3. **Situational Synergy** — the environment or story context favors you.

**Important:** You may benefit from **one** synergy bonus for **+2**, or **two** synergy bonuses for **Advantage** (instead of a bigger flat bonus). This rule encourages teamwork and clever use of the environment.

---

## When Do You Get Synergy?

### 1. Cooperative Synergy (+2)
- Granted when another **trained ally** (or a relevant tool in someone else’s hands) meaningfully assists in your attempt.  
- **Example:** A rogue climbs a wall with a rope that a fighter is holding steady. The fighter’s physical support grants the rogue **+2** to the climb check.

### 2. Situational Synergy (+2)
- Granted when a **favorable environment** or in-world factor directly aids your check.  
- **Example:** Persuading a merchant in their favorite tavern while picking up the tab. The relaxed atmosphere and hospitality grant **+2** to the Persuasion check.

---

## Stacking Synergy
- If **two distinct** synergy factors apply (e.g., you’re getting both Cooperative and Situational Synergy), you roll with **Advantage** instead of a total +4.
- You **cannot stack** synergy with other sources of Advantage. If you already have Advantage from a class feature, you don’t get an additional +2 or some form of “super Advantage.”

---

## Base DC of 16 (Levels 1–4)
Under these rules, most **challenging skill checks** for 1st-tier characters start at **DC 16**:

- A single synergy bonus (**+2**) effectively brings the target number closer to **14**.
- Two synergy bonuses (**Advantage**) significantly improves your odds.

This higher DC ensures the challenges remain interesting but rewards **creative teamwork**.

---

## Sample Scenarios

### 1. Picking a Lock (Cooperative Synergy)
- A rogue with Thieves’ Tools tries to pick a complicated lock (**DC 16**).  
- A wizard proficient in Thieves’ Tools (or with relevant expertise) gives steady guidance.  
- Because another **proficient** character is meaningfully helping, the rogue’s lockpicking check gets **+2**.  
- Success opens the lock; failure means they’ll need a new plan or risk complications.

### 2. Quiet Negotiations (Situational Synergy)
- A bard attempts to negotiate peace between two rival merchants (**DC 16**).  
- The bard leads them to a comfortable, neutral inn and treats them to a private booth and warm drinks.  
- The **calming environment** grants **+2** (Situational Synergy) to the bard’s Persuasion check.  
- If the bard also brought a respected mediator (Cooperative Synergy), the bard would roll with **Advantage** instead of another +2.

### 3. Scaling a Cliff (Cooperative + Situational)
- A ranger wants to climb a steep cliff (**DC 16**).  
- A druid **clears loose rocks** (Situational Synergy for safer footing), while the fighter holds a rope (Cooperative Synergy).  
- Two synergy factors = the ranger climbs with **Advantage** on the Athletics check.

---

## Tips for Players

### 1. Ask if Synergy Applies
- Before rolling, **explain narratively** how an ally or the environment is helping. If the DM agrees, you get your **+2** or **Advantage**.

### 2. One Synergy vs. Two
- **One synergy bonus** = **+2**  
- **Two synergy bonuses** = **Advantage**  
- No triple-stacking, and **no synergy** if you already have another form of Advantage.

### 3. Stay Creative
- This rule **rewards imaginative teamwork**.  
- Even small details—like **cleaning a surface** before forging a seal—could provide a Situational bonus if it **makes a tangible in-game difference**.

---

## Final Word
This streamlined approach keeps skill checks **exciting** and fosters **cooperation** without piling on too many small modifiers. By setting a base **DC of 17** for 1st-tier challenges and offering synergy as **+2** or **Advantage**, you maintain tension while giving players multiple avenues for success.
