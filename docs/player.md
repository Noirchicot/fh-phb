The Companion is docked on the right of this page — and it stays with you on every
page of the handbook. Collapse it with the arrow in its header; the gold **FH** seal
in the bottom-right corner brings it back.

## What lives in the dock

| Zone | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Vitals** | The six abilities and their saves. Click the score for a check, the `SV` strip for a save. |
| **Skills & Tools** | All 26 Fate's Hand checks plus the tools you actually bought. A row rolls flat; the gear opens the Roll Console. |
| **Destiny** | Current Points, maximum score and the die pool. A pool die can power a prepared roll — or rescue one that already landed. |
| **Roll Console** | Advantage/disadvantage, the fixed FH +2, a manual modifier, Guidance, Bardic and Destiny. `⋯` reveals Portents and per-die advantage. |
| **Roller & Tray** | The dice themselves. With a console open the tray adds bonus dice to that roll (three maximum); with no console it is a free damage pool. |
| **Stream** | Every finished roll, named and fully broken down. |

Your character, Destiny state and roll history are stored per campaign, so the dock
picks up where you left off.

## Getting your character in

Open the menu (`⋮`) → **Change character**, enter your campaign code and pick your
name. From the same menu you can **Sync D&D Beyond**, **Edit sheet** to correct
imported values, or jump to the full-screen Inventory and Soulforge pages.

D&D Beyond remains the source for standard D&D data. This dock owns the Fate's Hand
layer: Destiny, the Soulforging loop and the party inventory.
