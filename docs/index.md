# Fate's Hand — Player's Handbook

> *"There are more worlds than you know. Tread carefully, lest you rouse the powers that shape them."*

**Fate's Hand 5+** is a house-ruled expansion of **D&D 2024 (PHB 2024)**. It keeps everything you know and layers three things on top:

- **A wider skill system** — 26 skills (Perception split into Delve / Survival / Vigilance) with **tools folded into the same point pool**, so characters are more specialised and teamwork matters more.
- **The Fate's Hand mechanic** — a **Destiny Score** and **Destiny Dice** that let you bend fate at a price, with **Chaos** waiting when you overreach.
- **The Major Arcana** — a tarot-drawn destiny that grants a signature power.

…plus tightened rules for **combat, grappling, feats and exploration**.

## How to use this book

- **Building a character?** Start with **Creating a Character** and go top-to-bottom. The interactive **[Skill-Point Builder](builder.md)** is your companion for the Skills & Tools step.
- **At the table?** **Playing the Game** holds the systems you'll actually use; **Reference** has the flip-to-it tables.
- **📖 markers** flag the *complex* skills/tools — they have extra rules worth a read.

!!! note "Base rules"
    Anything not changed here works exactly as in **D&D 2024**. This handbook only documents the **Fate's Hand** differences.
