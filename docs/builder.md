# Skill-Point Builder

Use this tool to distribute your Fate's Hand skill & tool points: set your **class, level and species**, then spend your pool across the tiers (**½ / Prof / Exp**). Click any **📖** for the complex-rule popups, and use **⬇ Download payload** to export your choices.

[Open the builder full-screen ↗](../skill-builder.html){ .md-button target=_blank }

!!! tip "Pushing to D&D Beyond"
    Distribute your points here and **download your payload**, then send the `.json` to your GM — they run the writer that creates the skills/tools on your D&D Beyond sheet.
