Fate's Hand

CHARACTER BUILDER · LEVEL 1

    Who are you, and at whose table do you sit?

    Fate's Hand rule — roll 3d6 ten times, keep your six highest. If no result is 15 or higher, the whole set is reforged. At creation an ability can't exceed 18, bonuses included; in play the ceiling is 20, boons excepted.

    Scores rolled at the table? Enter them manually

    Your species' traits appear on the sheet as you choose.

    The badge is your level-1 skill-point pool (background included).

    Bard gains +1 point per level from level 2 (Jack of All Trades). Every class gains +2 points at levels 4 and 8.

    Your sheet's background is replaced by Blank Background (fh). It gives your ability bonuses, your Origin feat and your story.

    Destiny Touched (fh) and Skilled feed directly into character creation (Destiny score / skill points).

    Destiny is a random draw among the 22 Major Arcana — let fate decide, or set the card you drew at the table.

    Rule reminders
    • Starting floor = what you already own (species, imported sheet) — you can never invest below it. Invested tier = where your points go now.
    • Languages: free at creation. Later levels: 1 point = 1 language (GM options).
    • Each level: you may move 1 point — keeping at least ½ on every native skill and tool.
    • Levels 4 and 8: +2 points. Costs — half 1 · proficient 2 · expertise 4 (expertise purchase from level 4).
    • At least 1 tool point at creation. Clear every warning before saving — a silent leftover is how a skill ends up at zero.
