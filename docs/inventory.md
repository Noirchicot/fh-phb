# Party Inventory

The **shared loot ledger** of your campaign: everything the party hauls out of dungeons lands here. Move an item to a player when it's claimed, delete it when it's consumed or sold, and jump to the **Soulforge** when it's monster-part o'clock.

Everyone at the table sees the same list — it lives on the campaign server, keyed by your **campaign code**.
