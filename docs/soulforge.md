# Soulforge Workshop

Track your **harvested parts, gems, Soulgems and catalysts**, assemble them under the [three laws of equilibrium](chapters/soulforge-crafting.md#the-three-laws-of-equilibrium), and make the **Soulforging check** — mishaps, boons and growth included. Every forger keeps their own inventory; the **Party Stash** is shared.

!!! tip "Rules & D&D Beyond"
    The full system lives in the [Soulforge Crafting](chapters/soulforge-crafting.md) chapter. A finished item can be **exported as JSON**, or copied as a request for Claude's **ddb-homebrew** skill to create it on D&D Beyond.

!!! warning "Your data lives in this browser"
    Inventories are saved in your browser (localStorage). Use **Backup** in the workshop header to copy your data to another device.
