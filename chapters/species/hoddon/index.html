    oddon¶

    Most people on Nymedes have never seen a Hoddon, and plenty insist nobody has. The little folk keep to hidden family-holds deep in the old forests, guarding what remains of the banned craft of the vanished humans — remnants the Empire of Huitzlika has hunted for centuries, and the Hoddon along with them. A Hoddon on the road is a rare thing: quiet, watchful, and carrying more forbidden knowledge than any imperial archive.

    They live under willows. A grown weeping willow of the Demon Forest — a Sauleur — roofs a space some ten metres across, and a hold is a dozen or so relatives beneath one of them, related by blood and by the one craft they will not discuss. They keep the Magician among the Arcana, which the Empire finds insolent and the Hoddon find self-evident.

    The craft is the reason they are hunted, and it is not metaphor. Reaching adulthood, a Hoddon gives up an eye and takes a grey orb in its place. What the orb opens onto is a matrix laid over the visible world, and inside it sits a great deal of what the vanished humans knew. Nothing is written down. There are no Hoddon books to seize, no archive to burn, and no way for an outsider to read a captured orb — the fusion is a Hoddon technique and it has never left the holds. Imperial inquisitors have been trying for three centuries and have, so far, obtained eyes.

    What comes out of the craft is constructs. Hoddon-made things guard a boundary or run an orchard without supervision; some are shaped like forest animals, some like small forge-workers, some like the clockwork Modrons of an older cosmology. A hold that has lost half its people will still have its fields brought in.

    They do not replace their losses easily, and they will tell you why without being asked. Hoddon children go missing, and when one does, the hold says the heron took it. A white heron comes down through the willow crown at dusk, the story goes, and carries the child up to heaven, because the child was too good for this world and heaven would not wait. Every hold has lost some. Every hold tells it in the same words, with the same small nod at the end that closes the subject.

    It is a story told to children by adults who need it more than the children do. Anyone who has spent a winter under a Sauleur notices what the telling is for: nobody ever asks which child, the names are not spoken again, and a Hoddon who has drunk enough will change the subject with a speed that has nothing to do with grief. Holds disagree about the herons too — bitterly, along lines nobody will explain — and two holds that disagree do not visit one another.

    There is shame under it, and the shame is old. Whatever the herons are, the Hoddon have been telling it to themselves for long enough that most of them believe it by the time they are grown, and the ones who do not have learned to nod at the right moment.

    So they are few, they are careful, and they are very good at not being where the search is. Their neighbours mistake the caution for timidity. Anyone who has watched a Hoddon read a room with one living eye knows better.

    Traits

    Creature type Humanoid · Size Small (about 3–4 feet tall) · Speed 30 feet

    • Darkvision — 60 feet.
    • Hoddon Cunning — Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.
    • Hoddon Lineage — choose one from the table below. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this trait (chosen with the lineage).
    • Destiny FH — Base 2.

    Hoddon Lineages

    Lineage Level 1
    Forest Folk You know Minor Illusion, and always have Speak with Animals prepared — castable without a slot 2 times (+1 at levels 5, 9, 13 and 17) per Long Rest
    Rock Folk You know Mending and Prestidigitation, and can spend 10 minutes to build a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 HP) carrying one Prestidigitation effect; up to three at a time
    The Mole People FH Your Darkvision increases to 120 feet; you gain Meticulous — Advantage on Investigation checks — and 1 skill point (Novice) in tinker's tools

    The Hoddon are the Fate's Hand name for the base game gnome. Base the base game text: Gnome
